The law says otherwise:

49 U.S.C. § 42305(a)In the case of a passenger that holds a nonrefundable ticket on a scheduled flight to, from, or within the United States, an air carrier or a foreign air carrier shall, upon request as set forth in subsection (f), provide a full refund, including any taxes and ancillary fees, for the fare such carrier collected for any cancelled flight or significantly delayed or changed flight where the passenger chooses not to— (1) fly on the significantly delayed or changed flight or accept rebooking on an alternative flight;

Our rule left that condition out. It was wrong on 21 of 130 cases, the same way every time. A wrong rule in code fails consistently.

With the rule fixed

We rewrote the rule to match the law's words and decided the same 130 cases again, with no new model call. The fix changes only 20 runs: two cases that lack a fact the rule needs now go to a person instead of getting a refund. The AI still gathers the facts, explains the decision and hands unclear cases to a person. The rule decides.

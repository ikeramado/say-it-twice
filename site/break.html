Demo: five cases that fail on purpose

A delay at the boundary

Case test-bnd-005 (type boundary). Arrival delay 180 min.

The delay sits next to the rule's delay threshold, as 2 h 59 min sits against 3 h 01 min: a threshold read on the wrong side, or the wrong version's threshold, flips the answer.

A renumbered flight

Case test-ren-001 (type renumbered). Arrival delay 16 min.

The airline changed the flight's number. Whether that is a cancellation depends on the rule version in force and on the customer's choice, so an agent that decides without the conditions gives a wrong answer.

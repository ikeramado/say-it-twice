System

Group A: A purchase date in a policy gap, read two ways (cause a)

No saved copy of American's contract covers the purchase date. The engine weighs the contract versions that ADR-0005 names and finds one whose text decides; the label follows the human's labeling criteria, under which an uncovered purchase date leaves the airline's conditions unknown, and escalates. The engine and the label read the same gap differently.

Group B: A label wrong by its own criterion (cause d)

The contract text in force at purchase no longer holds the flight-number sentence the label's criterion rests on, so the engine's decision follows the text and the label does not. The label stays frozen and the registered figures stand.

  • test-ren-008 (type renumbered): 10 critical runs

Group C: The flight-number sentence read without its condition (cause a)

The engine reads American's sentence that a flight number change is a cancellation as a refund and drops the condition the same clause sets (the customer chooses not to fly). The label escalates on that condition, and the text supports the label.

  • test-ren-010 (type renumbered): 10 critical runs

The facts BTS does not hold are constructed for this page and marked so: each purchase date is 30 days before its flight, the passenger accepts no alternative, and whether the flight was renumbered is set per example. The airline policy is Delta's version for that purchase date and does not move with the date; only the date, and with it the rule version in force, does. Every decision is the rule engine's, computed when the page was built.

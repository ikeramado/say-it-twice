Technical Reporting Directives

The Department’s Bureau of Transportation Statistics (BTS) Office of Airline Information issues periodic Accounting and Reporting Directives (A&RD) and industry letters that serve the reporting air carriers by providing clarity to the regulations. Accounting and Reporting Directives addressing Airline Service Quality Performance statistics (On-time performance, Mishandled Baggage and Wheelchairs and Scooters, and Oversales data) are available at www.bts.gov/accounting-reporting-directives.

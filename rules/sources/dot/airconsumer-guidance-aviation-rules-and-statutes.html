Guidance On Aviation Rules and Statutes

Below is a compilation of guidance documents related to the Department of Transportation’s aviation economic, consumer protection, and civil rights requirements.  The guidance documents contained herein lack the force and effect of law and are not meant to bind the public in any way. The guidance documents are intended only to provide clarity to the public regarding existing requirements under the law or agency policies.

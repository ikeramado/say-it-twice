## **Your rights under US law**

For your American Airlines flight **AA28 (Los Angeles LAX → New York JFK), scheduled for March 8, 2025**, which arrived 146 minutes (2 hours and 26 minutes) late, the general rule is:

No automatic cash compensation

**US federal law does not generally require airlines to pay passengers monetary compensation for domestic flight delays**, even when the airline is responsible for the delay.

This is different from the European Union, where Regulation EC 261/2004 can provide fixed compensation for qualifying delays of three hours or more.

However, you may have other rights depending on the circumstances.

### **1. Refund of your ticket**

Under the US Department of Transportation's refund rules, a domestic flight is considered significantly delayed when its scheduled arrival is pushed back by **3 hours or more**.

|US domestic significant-delay threshold|3h|

- You did not reach the federal three-hour threshold.

- You are not entitled to a ticket refund under the significant-delay rule.

- Even if the delay had exceeded three hours, accepting the delayed flight and travelling to your destination would generally eliminate your right to a refund under that rule.


Sources: DOT refund rules and 14 CFR Part 260.

### **2. Monetary compensation for the delay**

Unfortunately, US law does not establish a fixed compensation amount for domestic flight delays.

There is no statutory entitlement to:

- $250, $400 or $600 compensation.

- A percentage of your ticket price.

- Compensation calculated per hour of delay.

- Automatic cash compensation simply because the airline caused the delay.


This applies even when the delay results from circumstances within the airline's control.

### **3. Meals, accommodation and other expenses**

American Airlines has its own customer-service commitments.

For airline-controlled delays, its commitments include:

- **Meals:** Food or beverage credit when the delay is at least three hours after scheduled departure.

- **Hotels:** Accommodation if an airline-controlled delay requires an overnight stay away from your city of residence.

- **Transportation:** Transportation to and from the hotel when applicable.


These are airline commitments, rather than statutory cash compensation.

An important distinction: your 146-minute figure measures arrival delay, whereas American's meal commitment refers to the departure delay. If your departure was delayed by three hours or more, you could potentially qualify for a meal credit even though your arrival delay was shorter.

### **4. Can you claim damages?**

There is a distinction between automatic compensation and recovering actual losses.

If the delay caused you measurable financial losses, such as a missed prepaid reservation or another expense, you could attempt to pursue a contractual claim against American Airlines.

However:

- You would need evidence of the loss and its connection to the delay.

- Recovery is not automatic.

- American Airlines' Conditions of Carriage limit its responsibility for certain incidental and consequential damages.


Consequently, this is not a guaranteed avenue for compensation.

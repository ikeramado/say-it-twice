The specific entitlements under federal law and American Airlines' policies for a domestic flight cancellation include:

### 1. Ticket Refund (Forfeited Upon Rebooking)

- **The Law:** Under DOT rules, if an airline cancels your flight for any reason, you are entitled to a full cash refund to your original payment method—**only if you choose not to travel** and decline the airline's rebooking offers.

- **Your Situation:** By accepting the alternative flight, you accepted the airline's fulfillment of the contract of carriage, which forfeits your right to a fare refund.

### 2. Financial Compensation for Inconvenience

- **The Law:** Unlike the European Union (EU261), **U.S. federal law does not require airlines to pay cash compensation** for flight cancellations or delays, regardless of how long the delay is or whether it was the airline's fault. _(Mandatory cash compensation in the U.S. only applies if you are involuntarily bumped from an oversold flight)._

    Squaremouth


### 3. Exceptions Where You _Are_ Owed Money

Even though you took the rebooked flight, you are still legally entitled to a refund for **unprovided ancillary services or class downgrades**:

- **Paid Extras Not Delivered:** If you paid extra on your original flight for seat selection (e.g., Main Cabin Extra or a preferred seat), Wi-Fi, or priority boarding, and those services were not provided on your alternative flight, the DOT requires American Airlines to refund those fees.

- **Cabin Downgrade:** If you originally purchased a First Class ticket and were placed in Main Cabin (Economy) on the rebooked flight, you are entitled to a refund of the fare difference.

- **Delayed Baggage:** If you checked a bag and it was delayed by more than **12 hours** after your domestic flight arrived (and you filed a mishandled baggage report), you are entitled to a refund of your checked bag fee.


### 4. Airline-Specific Commitments (Meals & Hotels)

While not a strict federal payout requirement, American Airlines has legally enforceable commitments on file with the DOT's Airline Customer Service Dashboard if the cancellation was **within the airline's control** (e.g., mechanical issues or crew shortages, rather than weather or Air Traffic Control):

- **Meals:** A meal voucher or reimbursement if the alternative flight caused you to wait **3 or more hours** past your original scheduled departure.

- **Hotel & Ground Transit:** A hotel voucher (or reimbursement for reasonable hotel costs) and transportation to/from the hotel if the cancellation forced an **overnight stay**.

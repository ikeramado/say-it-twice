## **3. What American Airlines additionally promised**

There is another distinction between federal legal entitlements and American Airlines' own customer-service commitments.

American's commitments for controllable cancellations included:

- Rebooking on another American flight at no additional cost.

- Rebooking on a partner airline at no additional cost.

- A meal or meal voucher when the cancellation resulted in a wait of three hours or more for a new flight.

- Hotel accommodation if the cancellation required an overnight stay.

- Ground transportation between the hotel and airport.


These are airline commitments, not automatic statutory compensation rights. Their applicability depends on the circumstances, particularly whether the cancellation was within American's control.

## **4. An important correction about the effective dates**

The distinction is slightly more nuanced than simply saying the new rule did not apply.

The FAA Reauthorization Act of 2024 was signed on **May 16, 2024**. Its refund provision, now codified at **49 U.S.C. § 42305**, took effect that day.

Therefore, it was already in force when your flight was cancelled on June 2, 2024. It expressly provides a refund when a passenger chooses not to accept alternative transportation. Since you accepted American's replacement flight, that provision does not ordinarily give you a refund.
